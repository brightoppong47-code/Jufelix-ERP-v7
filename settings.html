Reset Business Data

Start your operational records again without removing your administrator login, company information, logo or theme.

  • Products and inventory
  • Sales
  • Purchases
  • Expenses
  • Transfers
  • Customers
  • Suppliers
  • Branch records
  • Stock ledger
  • Supplier payments
Your Firebase Authentication account and administrator login will be preserved. Company settings, logo and theme will also remain unchanged.

Factory Reset ERP

Completely reset the ERP business environment and return it to a fresh configuration.

  • All operational business records
  • Company information
  • Company logo
  • Theme preferences
  • Currency and receipt preferences
Your Firebase Authentication account will still be preserved so you are not locked out of the ERP.
